## The Project

Heart Hotel is a proposed heart-shaped hotel and casino on the former SkyVue site, on the east side of Las Vegas Boulevard South directly across from Mandalay Bay. Our record provenance is a pair of Clark County entitlement filings by the applicant of record, Kulik River Capital, LLC, heard by the Clark County Zoning Commission on July 22, 2026. [PRESS] County commissioners approved the project unanimously that day.

#### Design and program (press-reported)
- [PRESS] A 29-story tower with 752 rooms, described as a heart-shaped hotel and casino on roughly 12 acres across from Mandalay Bay.
- [PRESS] The tower's heart-shaped glass curtain wall is wrapped in a full-height LED display.
- [PRESS] Program includes more than 42,000 square feet of convention space, a gaming floor, and a covered pedestrian plaza built on the Fremont Street Experience model.
- [PRESS] A 265-foot sky restaurant is planned atop the reinforced columns left standing from the SkyVue project.
- [PRESS] The tower was reduced by 60 feet from an earlier 874-room scheme to satisfy FAA height compliance.
- [PRESS] The parcel was purchased in spring 2026 for 70 million dollars.

Note the alignment between record and press: the record's expansion of the gaming enterprise district, resort hotel, and recreational facility on 11.95 acres in a Commercial Resort zone across from Mandalay Bay is the entitlement basis for the press-described 752-room heart-shaped hotel-casino on roughly 12 acres.

## The Opportunity
- [JKR ASSESSMENT] A full-height LED curtain wall on a 29-story Strip tower, combined with a covered plaza canopy, would be among the largest LED display packages entitled in Las Vegas since the Sphere.
- [JKR ASSESSMENT] The project is entitled but pre-financing and pre-design-package. That is precisely the window in which a large-scale LED specialist should be introduced to the developer and the architect, before scopes are let.
- [JKR ASSESSMENT] Recommended door: the land-use representative who carried the entitlement, as the warm path to the principal and the design team.

## Status and Risk

[JKR ASSESSMENT] This is an early-stage opportunity by definition. The county approval permits the design, but financing, construction permitting, and contractor selection all remain ahead, and the site carries a failed-project history from the never-completed SkyVue development that stalled on the same parcel. A referral now is a relationship-timing play into an entitled but unfinanced project, not a signed scope, and it should be weighed as such.
