## Why this order

Brief Q established what the register holds: 340 live projects, 45 hospitality
developments, 15 that could carry a brief today, and 116 that should not be in
the register at all. 94 of those 116 are housekeeping and 73 of those are
Broward comprehensive-plan items.

Removing one gate term takes a third of the junk out. That is the cheapest
quality gain available and it is measured, blocked only on a stale corpus.

Everything else in this brief is readiness: what happens when the system runs
without someone watching it.

## Standing rules

Measure before you build. A rule ships with the count behind it and the cost
per market stated. Report before you propose. Read the body, not the status
code. Never fabricate. Nothing is silently absent. Sweep for the shape before
the fix is committed. State the cap beside any figure taken from a capped read.
NPM_EXIT from the captured line on every gate.

going live on 2026-08-19, and it predates the Legistar paging fix that replaced
the newest-200-by-id read with a date window paged to exhaustion.

corpus, and the Broward change cannot be costed on it.

- Both halves of any before-and-after must come from the NEW corpus. Taking

  pushing through.

Report the harvest result before measuring anything with it.

## ITEM 4. READINESS

The system runs when someone tells it to and someone reads the output. Report
what happens when neither is true.

Four questions, each measured or reasoned from the code, not guessed:

**A source dies mid-week.** A feed that 403s, times out, or starts serving an
error page with a 200. What does the run do, what does the register show, and
what does a document generated the next day say about it. Report which of those
three states is silent.

**Two generations at once.** Two documents built for the same client at the same
time, or a capture run and a generation overlapping. Report what shares state
and what would collide.

**The wrong report.** What stops a document generated for one client being
delivered as another's. The brand, the addressee and the membership gate are
three separate values and at least one has been wrong before.

**Cost at ten clients.** What one weekly cycle costs today in model calls,
requests and wall time, and what ten clients would cost. State the cap beside
any figure taken from a capped read.

Report all four. Build nothing in item 4.
